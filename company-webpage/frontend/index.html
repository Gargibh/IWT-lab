About Meridian Corp

Meridian Corp built this internal portal to replace scattered spreadsheets with one secure system - every employee record, in one place, accessible only to the people who should see it.

What the portal does

Built for both employees managing their own details, and HR managing everyone's.

1

Self-service profiles

Employees register, log in, and keep their own contact and personal details current at any time.

2

Centralized admin dashboard

HR can view, search, edit, and manage every employee record from a single screen.

3

Secure by design

Passwords are hashed, sessions are protected, and admin tools are only reachable by admins.
