    About Our Department

    Our story, our purpose, our people

    Department Overview

    Founded in 1998, the Department of Computer Science began with a single lab and a handful of faculty determined to bring rigorous computing education to the region. Over the past two decades, it has grown into one of the college's flagship departments, producing engineers, researchers, and entrepreneurs who now work across the globe.

    Today, the department blends a strong theoretical foundation with practical, industry-aligned training — preparing students not just to use technology, but to build it.

    Our Mission

    • Deliver a rigorous, current, and industry-relevant computing curriculum.
    • Foster research, innovation, and critical thinking among students.
    • Build strong industry partnerships for internships and placements.
    • Cultivate ethical, socially responsible technologists.

    Our Vision

    • Be recognized as a center of excellence in computer science education.
    • Produce graduates who lead innovation across industry and academia.
    • Expand research output in AI, systems, and data science.
    • Create an inclusive, collaborative learning environment for all.

    Achievements Timeline

    Milestones over the years

    1998

    Department Founded

    The Department of Computer Science was established with its first batch of 40 students.

    2008

    First Research Grant

    Received its first government-funded research grant for work in distributed computing.

    2015

    NBA Accreditation

    The BCA and B.Sc CS programs received national accreditation for academic quality.

    2021

    Innovation Center Launched

    Opened a dedicated innovation center for student startups and prototyping.

    2026

    AI & ML Lab Inaugurated

    A new GPU-equipped AI and Machine Learning lab opened to support advanced coursework and research.
